### Added — Phase 6 (payments, refunds, COD, reconciliation) — in progress
- Slice 10 — Phase 6 gate: **payment signals and alerts** (`PaymentHealth`, `CheckPaymentHealth`, every 5 minutes): callback failure rate (20 percent of at least 5 messages in 15 minutes), payments open 15 minutes past their limit, open amount/currency mismatches, refunds failed or stuck, messages that failed the gateway's own check (3 in 15 minutes); each breach is an ERROR log line `payments.alert` (once an hour per signal) and a banner on the finance pages; thresholds in `config/payments.php`. Browser tests (Playwright + axe, English and Bangla) for the customer pages: sign in, order, My orders, order page, cancel, and the confirmation page's account sign-up. Go-live steps for payments documented in DEPLOYMENT_AND_DEVOPS §5.2; Phase 6 decisions recorded as ADR P6-1…P6-18. Tests: 11 feature tests; two safeguards (the threshold comparison, the once-an-hour dedupe) removed once to prove tests fail without them.
- Slice 9 — customer pages (Orders; small contract in IdentityAccess), English and Bangla: **My orders** in the account menu (list, newest first, 10 per page, no extra queries for longer lists; order page with items, totals, delivery address, the shop's customer-visible messages — internal notes never — and a signed **Pay now / Pay again** link for an unpaid online order); **cancel my order** only while it is pending, unshipped, and no payment is taken or on its way (everything else is for the shop; refunds stay a person's decision); another customer's order, a guest order and an unknown order are all simply "not found". **After ordering**: the signed confirmation page offers a guest to make an account (the order goes into it; same answer whether or not the address already had an account, so addresses cannot be probed; the verification email is sent as usual) and a signed-in customer to add the order to their account. The signed link is the proof of ownership. `orders.user_id` can now be set once from NULL (database trigger `orders_owner_set_once`: an owner never changes and an owned order never becomes a guest order); the rest of the order stays frozen. New contract `IdentityAccess\Contracts\CustomerRegistration` (also carries the password rule, so the sign-up rule is used everywhere). Not built: claiming older guest orders by verified phone number (needs a decision on recycled numbers). Tests: 25 feature tests and 4 real multi-process tests (6 customers claiming one guest order → exactly one; 5 cancels → one; non-owners cannot cancel); four safeguards removed once each to prove a test fails without them.
- Slice 8 — admin screens for money (Payments; small additions to Orders and IdentityAccess), English and Bangla, all through the same Actions as everything else: **Money today** (online paid, cash collected, refunded today in the shop's Dhaka day; refunds waiting; payments needing review; open reconciliation items; cash-on-delivery aging); **Payments** list (search by order number, payment id, our reference or bank id; filter by state; no extra queries for longer lists) and **payment page** (attempts, refunds with who asked and who approved, reconciliation items, the gateway's raw notifications with secrets, card numbers, phone numbers and verification signatures hidden and the stored copy never changed, "ask the payment service again", refund request form); **Refunds** queue (approve, reject with reason, send, mark paid back by hand with proof); **Cash on delivery** (aging, record cash by order number, enter a courier statement from the list of collected cash); **Reconciliation** (open items with filters, close with a note, upload a settlement file, history of files checked); **Payment settings** (pause/resume online payments, refund second-approver limit, which methods are offered, provider health from our own records). Permissions: view = `payments.view`; refunds = `payments.refund`; cash, statements, reconciliation, ask-again and the pause switch = `payments.reconcile`; limit and methods = `settings.manage`. Each upload/action has its own rate limit per person and per route (Laravel's `throttle:N,M` shares one counter per person across all routes, which let unrelated screens eat each other's allowance). New small contracts: `Orders\Contracts\OrderDirectory::findByNumber`, `IdentityAccess\Contracts\StaffDirectory` (names only). `Redactor` now also hides `verify_sign` / `verify_key`. Tests: 28 feature tests (permission matrix for every route, both languages, every flow through HTTP, redaction, query count, escaping, rate limit); permission checks, redaction and the pause switch were each removed once to prove a test fails without them.
- Slice 7 — reconciliation (Payments): (1) **scheduled check** every 10 minutes (`ReconcilePendingPayments`): online attempts still unfinished after 10 minutes (`payments.reconcile_after_minutes`) are put to the gateway, so a payment whose notification never arrived is still found; a payment still open 2 hours (`payments.long_pending_hours`) after its time limit is raised to finance as `long_pending`. (2) **Settlement file check** (`ImportSettlement`, table `settlement_imports`): a plain CSV `reference,bank_txn_id,amount,currency,settled_at` for one provider and a period of at most 31 days; a malformed file is refused as a whole with the line numbers; the same file is checked once (hash UNIQUE, also against two uploads at once). A line we do not know → `unmatched_provider_txn`; a different amount → `amount_mismatch`; currency → `currency_mismatch`; a different bank id → `unmatched_provider_txn`; our paid attempts verified inside the period that the file omits → `orphan_payment`. **A file never makes anything paid**: a line that says paid while we show unpaid is put to the gateway and only its answer counts. This is NOT a provider-specific format: no saved provider document describes a settlement file yet; mapping a real SSLCommerz report needs its documentation saved first. (3) **Closing exceptions** (`ResolveReconciliationException`): resolved or ignored, a note required, the first person to close wins, audited, moves no money. Each problem is raised once (UNIQUE type + reference). Tests: 25 feature tests and 2 real multi-process tests (5 simultaneous uploads of one file → one check, 3 exceptions; 6 people closing one exception → one); each safeguard was removed once to prove a test fails without it. Permission `payments.reconcile` and the screens arrive in slice 8.
- Slice 6 — cash on delivery: collection, courier statements, aging (Payments; small `CodOrders` contract in Orders): staff record that the cash for a COD order was collected (the amount is always the order's total, so it cannot be mistyped; once per order, enforced by a database UNIQUE so two people recording the same delivery at once cannot both succeed; date cannot be in the future or before the order). The cash becomes a normal paid `payments` row (provider `cod`) with one succeeded attempt, so refunds work exactly as for online money (always by hand with proof), and the order follows through the outbox (payment status `paid`; the order's progress is not moved). Courier statements: staff enter a statement (courier, statement number, amount received, the parcels it lists with what the courier says it paid for each); the expected total is what OUR collections add up to; the statement is `reconciled`, `short` or `over` (a `cod_short` / `cod_over` exception for finance), or `pending` when the total agrees but a parcel does not (`amount_mismatch` exception). A statement can be entered once per courier and a parcel can sit on one statement only (database UNIQUEs). Aging report (`CodAging`): cash not yet collected (confirmed/processing/completed COD orders) and cash collected but not yet remitted, in 0-3 / 4-7 / 8-14 / 15+ day buckets. Migrations add `cod_collections.payment_id` and a unique marker so an order has one hand-recorded collection (each shipment, later, collects its own). Tests: 23 feature tests and 5 real multi-process tests (8 simultaneous recordings of one delivery → one; 6 statements claiming one parcel → one; 5 entries of one statement → one); each database guard was removed once to prove a test fails without it. Permission `payments.cod` is enforced at the routes in slice 8.
- Slice 5 — refunds (Payments, small change in Orders): staff **request** a refund (amount, reason, optionally for one specific attempt), a person **approves** it (a different person above ৳5,000, editable setting `payments.refund_second_approver_minor`) or **rejects** it (reason required), then it is **sent** to the gateway or, when the gateway cannot do it, **completed by hand with proof** (bank/bKash reference). Money given back can never pass money received: the amount is reserved on the payment with one conditional `UPDATE … WHERE refunded_minor + ? <= captured_minor` under the payment row lock (database CHECK as backstop), and a rejection or gateway refusal gives it back. A refund sent to the gateway carries our own unique `refund_ref`; "accepted/processing" stays `processing` until the gateway says refunded (scheduled `PollRefunds` every 5 minutes; unreachable gateway → same call repeated after 60 seconds; stuck over 3 days → `refund_stuck` exception). A second paid attempt on one payment (duplicate payment) can be refunded within that attempt's own amount. `payments.refund_succeeded` reaches the order through the outbox: full → `refunded`, partial → `partially_refunded`, extra/late → note only; Bangla/English SMS; a fully refunded order can now be cancelled. Migration adds `refunds.payment_transaction_id`. Tests: 38 feature tests and 7 real multi-process tests (12 simultaneous 10% requests → exactly 10 accepted; 6 × 60% → exactly 1; four simultaneous approvals → one; six simultaneous sends → the gateway asked once); each safeguard was removed once to prove a test fails without it.
- Slice 4 — the hostile-callback suite (tests and a probe command; no behaviour change except that nothing was found wrong): a 24-story **replay suite** (duplicates, the same payment through every channel at once, browser-before-notification and the reverse, failure-then-success in one session, cancel-then-success, late "failed" notices after payment, success after the time limit, after staff cancelled the order and four days after a failure, forged notifications, amounts too small / too large / one paisa out, another currency, another transaction's answer, no bank transaction id, a notification for a transaction that is not ours, gateway down then back, two attempts both paid) in which the same money rules are checked after EVERY step: never more captured than asked, no money without a verified attempt that has its bank id, paid exactly when the order is paid, at most one "paid" announcement per payment, a paid order is never also told it expired. **Real multi-process tests** (eight OS processes, own database connections, released together): eight simultaneous verifications → one payment taken and one announcement; two attempts both reported paid → money taken once, the extra flagged for refund; one notification delivered eight times at once → stored once, paid once; "failed" reports racing "paid" ones → always paid, never undone; success racing the expiry job → either paid, or expired AND recorded as a late payment (never lost, never invented); a committed-but-never-dispatched announcement (a crash) → delivered once by four racing workers and again harmlessly on redelivery. Each safeguard was removed in turn (row lock, notification de-duplication, amount and currency checks, "can this order still take the money") and the tests failed every time.
- Slice 3 — the SSLCommerz adapter (sandbox first), written only from the saved official documentation: starts a session (`POST /gwprocess/v4/api.php`) and sends the customer to the returned `GatewayPageURL` **only if it is an https address on sslcommerz.com** (a tampered answer cannot redirect a customer elsewhere); asks about a payment by validation id (Order Validation API) or by our own reference (Transaction Query API); requests refunds and reads refund status. A validation id that turns out to belong to another transaction is dropped and our own reference is asked instead (so borrowing someone else's paid id neither pays nor falsely alarms). Amounts are read exactly in paisa; when the two BDT amount fields disagree, or the text is not an exact amount, no amount is returned and the payment is held for a person. A high-risk rating still pays but holds the order for review instead of confirming it. `verify_sign` is not used (its recipe is not documented). Partial refunds are not declared (not shown in the documentation). The store password sits in the query string of the GET calls by SSLCommerz's own design, so an HTTP error is never logged or passed on as is: only the kind of failure is, and a test checks that the password and store id appear in no table row, outbox message, note or log line. **Fixed a flaw found while reading the docs:** SSLCommerz can report a failed try and then a success under the same transaction id (the query API returns several results per id), but an attempt closed as failed could never become paid, so a real payment would have been lost. A failed, cancelled or expired attempt may now still be found paid (the database allows exactly that one move; a paid attempt never changes), for a week after it started; more than one successful payment under one reference raises a "duplicate payment" for finance. The gateway is available only when credentials are set; the live bank needs production, sandbox off and `PAYMENTS_ALLOW_LIVE=true` all together, so a development machine holding live credentials cannot reach it. Tests now blank provider credentials and fail on any real HTTP request. Opt-in sandbox checks (not in CI) are in `app/Modules/Payments/tests/Sandbox/`.
- Slice 2 — paying an order online (Payments, Orders): "Pay online" appears at checkout only when the owner has switched it on, a gateway is set up, the payments freeze is off, and the order is inside the method's amount limits; downloads and services can now be bought. The order is placed unpaid with its stock held, then the customer is sent to the gateway. **A payment becomes "paid" in exactly one place (`VerifyPayment`)**: it asks the gateway server to server about OUR reference and requires the same reference, amount and currency (and a bank transaction id, without which a refund would be impossible) under the payment's row lock; anything else is held for a person as "needs review" and never paid; repeating it, or two at once, changes nothing. The browser return page and the gateway notification only say WHICH transaction to ask about: a forged "success" page or a forged "VALID" notification leaves the order unpaid (tested). Notifications are stored exactly as received (same one twice = stored once), answered at once and processed from a queue with back-off; return links are signed, expiring and exempt from CSRF only because they are. Retry after a failure is a new numbered attempt on the same payment (`VM-2610-000123-2`); pressing "pay" twice within two minutes gives the same gateway page rather than a second session; the database allows one open payment per order. Orders follow their payment only through outbox events (paid → paid and confirmed unless flagged; failed; expired → cancelled with stock released; mismatch → flagged with a note), each applied once. Unpaid online payments expire after 30 minutes (asking the gateway one last time first, so a customer who paid is never expired); money that arrives after expiry or for a cancelled order is recorded as a late payment for staff, never lost and never silently accepted. Cash orders nobody confirms are auto-cancelled after 3 days (setting `orders.pending_cancel_days`, 0 = never), freeing stock and telling the customer in their language. Order page shows paid / due / cancelled / refunded states with "Pay now" and "Check again". Online orders are not subject to the cash-order limits. Not yet: the real SSLCommerz adapter (slice 3); refunds (slice 5); admin screens (slice 8).
- Slice 1 — payment domain and gateway contract (new `Payments` module): tables `payment_methods` (+ translations), `payments`, `payment_transactions`, `payment_webhook_events`, `refunds`, `cod_collections`, `cod_remittances` (+ items), `reconciliation_exceptions`. The database refuses: amounts of zero or less, more received than asked, more refunded than received, rewriting what was agreed (amount, order, provider), deleting any payment record, the same bank transaction or notification stored twice, and any status change the state machine does not allow (a paid payment going back to pending, a final attempt changing again); a test runs every pair of statuses through the real triggers and compares with the enums so they cannot drift apart. `PaymentGateway` contract (initiate, parseCallback, verify, status, refund, refundStatus, capabilities) with `GatewayRegistry`, and a `FakeGateway` that plays any provider answer for tests and never touches a network. Provider decimal text is converted to paisa in one place with exact string arithmetic (`ProviderAmount`). Payment methods seeded once: cash on delivery (on) and "Pay online" via SSLCommerz (off until credentials are in). SSLCommerz's official documentation is saved in `docs/vendor/sslcommerz/` with what it does not say (the `verify_sign` recipe is unconfirmed, so authenticity rests on server-to-server validation).

Decisions and deviations recorded in the ADR addendum "Phase 5 decisions and deviations" (order number format, checkout inside Orders, stock release in the same transaction, the COD rules and their per-phone lock, hashes-only block list).
- Slice 9 — admin screens for what Phase 5 added (Shipping, Pricing, Orders; en / bn, permission-gated, audited): **delivery zones** (names, districts with "now in zone X" shown and an explicit move, one "everywhere else" zone that must stay active, cash on delivery per zone, price bands per method by weight in kg with an open "any heavier parcel" band, saved together or not at all), **delivery methods** (code fixed once made, names and delivery time in both languages, cash-on-delivery fee, free-over amount, on/off), **coupons** (percent with cap, fixed, free delivery; scope by SKU list or categories; minimum spend; total and per-customer limits that cannot go below what was used; Dhaka-time window; the code and the use count can never be edited) with a **usage report** (uses, total discount given, different customers, the orders that used it, linked for staff who may see orders), **automatic offers** (label in both languages, percent or fixed, minimum cart, window), and the **cash-order rules** screen (limits for the COD rules, effective from the next order) plus the **block list** (add a phone, switch entries off and on; hashes only). Staff with the `.view` permission can look at everything but change nothing; changing needs `shipping.manage`, `promotions.manage`, `settings.manage` and `orders.cancel` (block list) respectively.
- Slice 8 — cash-on-delivery abuse rules (Orders; small contract in IdentityAccess): a blocked phone or address is refused without saying why (only hashes are stored; staff block from the order page); at most 3 open cash orders per phone (pending, confirmed or processing), enforced under a per-phone database lock so eight simultaneous orders get through as exactly three; a cash order above ৳10,000 needs an SMS code (reuses the OTP service: single use, expiry, attempt cap, send limits; every wrong answer reads the same; confirmation is tied to that phone and expires after 30 minutes); many orders from one phone in a day, or one address used by 3 or more phones in 30 days, place the order but hold it for review (`needs_review`, reasons kept in append-only `order_fraud_flags`). A held order cannot be confirmed (and is never auto-confirmed) until staff mark it reviewed with a reason; it can still be cancelled. Limits are settings (`fraud.*`, defaults in `config/velmora.php`; editable screens come in slice 9). New: `order_blocklist`, `order_fraud_flags`, `order_addresses.fingerprint`, OTP purpose `checkout_cod`, `PhoneConfirmation` contract. Staff need `orders.update` to clear a review and `orders.cancel` to block a customer.
- Slice 7 — checkout (Orders, with small additions to Cart, Customers, Inventory): guest and signed-in checkout in three steps (details, review, confirmation) that work without JavaScript and in both languages. `PlaceOrder` does everything in ONE transaction: re-reads the cart, re-prices it (the total the shopper saw is only compared, never trusted), writes the order, holds the stock (one conditional UPDATE per line, no expiry for orders), uses the coupons, empties the cart and records the `OrderPlaced` event in the outbox; if anything fails, nothing stays. Submitting the same checkout twice, even at the same instant, gives the same order (UNIQUE `placement_key` from the form's one-time token). A price, code or stock change between the review page and the button shows the new total instead of charging a different one. Cash on delivery (fee from the delivery method); orders start `pending` (`ORDERS_AUTO_CONFIRM_COD` to confirm at once). Confirmation page via a signed, expiring link; SMS and email sent from the outbox (each at most once, retried separately; email only when given). Cancelling an order now releases its held stock in the same transaction. New contracts: `Inventory\StockReservations`, `Cart\CartWriter`, `Customers\AddressChecker`; `PaymentInitiator` stub for Phase 6. Customer address `delivery_note` kept on the order. Not yet: downloads and services cannot be ordered until online payment exists (Phase 6); "my orders" pages for signed-in customers.
- Slice 6 — orders core (new `Orders` module): `orders`, `order_items`, `order_addresses`, `order_status_history`, `order_tax_lines`, `order_notes`. An order is written exactly as priced (`CreateOrder`: breakdown must reconcile; lines, address and names are snapshots, so later catalog or price edits never change it) and numbered `VM-YYMM-NNNNNN` from a database sequence (month in Dhaka time; unique under concurrency). Totals are re-checked by a database CHECK; money, identity and time are frozen by triggers (only the three states, review flag and cancellation fields may change), nothing is deleted, history and notes are append-only. Three state machines (order status, payment, delivery) with allowed moves, cross-rules (no cancel after shipping or once money is held, complete only when delivered and paid, confirm before shipping) and a history row with actor and reason for every move; moves lock the order row so cancel-vs-ship races have exactly one winner. `coupon_redemptions.order_id` now points at real orders. Admin: order list (filters, search by number / phone / name, needs-review queue, fixed query count, phones masked without `customers.view_pii`) and order page (lines, totals, address, history timeline, state controls, internal / customer notes); cancelling needs `orders.cancel`. en / bn throughout. Doc change: order numbers are `VM-…`, not `ORD-…` (accepted in the Phase 5 plan).
- Slice 5 — coupons and promotions (Pricing, Cart): coupons (percent with optional cap, fixed amount, free delivery) limited by dates (end is exclusive), minimum spend measured on the eligible items only, total uses and uses per customer, and scope (everything, chosen products, or categories including sub-categories). One discount code plus one free-delivery code may be combined, never more. The amount is split over the eligible lines to the paisa. Automatic promotions: the best qualifying one applies, with a label in the shopper's language. Redemption is one atomic conditional `UPDATE` (usage limit also a database CHECK), idempotent per order, all-or-nothing, undone with the order's transaction, append-only `coupon_redemptions`. Cart: code box with plain-language refusals in en/bn, codes re-checked every time the cart is shown (a code that stops working is flagged, not silently charged), discount and free-delivery lines in the summary. Proven with real multi-process tests (10 orders racing for the last use → exactly one; per-customer limit from 8 tabs; same order twice). Admin screens follow in slice 9.
- Slice 4 — pricing engine (Pricing): `CartPricer` → `PriceBreakdown` (subtotal, line and order discounts, shipping, COD fee, tax, grand total). Pure arithmetic in `BreakdownCalculator` (no database, no settings): the order discount is split across lines to the paisa (`Money::allocate`), tax is taken once per discounted line, shipping and COD fee carry tax only when the owner says so, discounts are clamped to what they apply to. The identity `grand = subtotal − discounts + shipping + COD fee + (tax if prices exclude it)` is checked by `PriceBreakdown::reconciles()` and equals the `orders` CHECK. Totals can be recomputed later from stored line numbers alone. Tested with hand-checked examples and ~190,000 assertions over thousands of random orders.
- Slice 3 — shipping (new `Shipping` module, rates only): zones (districts, one fallback zone), global methods with per-zone weight-band prices, free-delivery threshold per method, free-shipping coupon hook, cash-on-delivery eligibility (zone and method) and flat fee, delivery-time text in both languages, nothing to ship for digital/service-only carts; `ShippingQuoter` contract (3 queries per quote), placeholder seed (Dhaka ৳60, elsewhere ৳120, free over ৳3,000, COD fee ৳0; never overwrites owner changes). Admin screens follow in slice 9. Cart lines now carry their weight.
- Slice 2 — cart (new `Cart` module): persistent cart for guests (random HttpOnly cookie, only its hash stored, 30 days) and customers (90 days), merge on sign-in (quantities add up, capped), add / change / remove with min, step, max and stock rules, "price changed" notice with an accept button, unavailable and low-stock warnings, totals always recomputed from live prices (sales included) and stock in a fixed number of queries (`PriceCatalog::quoteMany`), header cart link with count, expiry purge job, add-to-cart on the product page. New `PurchasableVariants` contract in Catalog.
- Slice 1 — geography and addresses: 8 divisions, 64 districts and 494 upazilas in English and Bangla (MIT-licensed open dataset; source, licence and date recorded in `app/Modules/Customers/Database/data/geography/SOURCE.md`; not an official BBS file, one upazila fewer than the official 495), customer address book (up to 10 addresses, one default enforced by the database, phone cleaned to +880 form, location chain validated on the server, ownership enforced), address form that works without JavaScript (every district and upazila listed under its parent) with an Alpine enhancement. `Geography` and `AddressBook` contracts for checkout and shipping.
